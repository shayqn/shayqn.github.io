HOB Privacy Policy

HOB is operated by its owner for personal household tasks.

HOB accesses provided and connected data to carry out owner-requested tasks. Relevant data may be processed on the operator’s infrastructure and by configured third-party AI providers, subject to those providers’ terms and privacy policies.

HOB does not sell Google user data or use it for advertising. Credentials and operational data may be stored on the operator’s infrastructure. The owner controls retention and deletion of HOB’s stored data.

The owner can revoke HOB’s access through their Google Account’s third-party connections settings. Revoking access does not delete previously stored data.
